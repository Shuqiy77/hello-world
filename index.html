About

Pianist and educator

Shuqi Yang is a pianist and teacher based in Florida. She performs as a solo recitalist, chamber musician and collaborative pianist, and brings strong sight-reading and sight-singing skills to every rehearsal.

She is completing her Master’s degree at the University of Central Florida, where she studies with Dr. Esther Jeehae Ahn and previously studied with Dr. Lindsay Garritson. She also serves as a teaching assistant, leading secondary piano group classes at four levels. She earned her Bachelor of Music at the Lynn University Conservatory of Music in Boca Raton on a full scholarship, studying with Dr. Roberta Rust.

Shuqi is fluent in English and Mandarin.
